Snowflake 25 - How to Play

Learn the rules of Hex and how to use this AI-powered game interface

🎯 Object of the Game

Hex is a strategic board game where two players compete to connect opposite sides of a hexagonal board.

  • Blue player (you): Connect the top and bottom edges
  • Red player (AI): Connect the left and right edges
  • First player to create an unbroken chain wins!

📋 Basic Rules

1. Taking Turns

Players alternate placing pieces on the hexagonal board. Blue always goes first.

2. Placing Pieces

Click on any empty hexagon to place your piece. Once placed, pieces cannot be moved or removed.

3. Winning

You win by creating a continuous path of your pieces from one edge to the opposite edge:

  • Blue wins: Connect top edge to bottom edge
  • Red wins: Connect left edge to right edge
💡 Pro Tip: In Hex, there are no ties! One player will always win due to the nature of the board geometry.

🥧 Pie Rule (Swap)

The pie rule is a balancing rule used after the first move.

  • Blue makes the first move.
  • Red then chooses either to continue as Red, or to swap and take over Blue.
  • In Snowflake 25, when pie rule is enabled, the AI can make this swap choice probabilistically on move 1.

If a swap happens, no extra stone is placed. Control/color assignment changes and play continues from the same board position.

🎮 How to Use This Interface

Making Moves

Simply click on any empty hexagon to place your piece. The game will automatically switch to the AI's turn.

Game Controls

  • Undo: Take back your last move
  • Redo: Restore a move you undid
  • Computer Move: Force the AI to make a move (useful when AI is disabled)
  • New Game: Start a fresh game
  • Save Game: Save the current game sequence

Difficulty Settings

Adjust the AI difficulty using the settings panel:

  • Novice (ELO 500): Very random play, makes many mistakes
  • Medium (ELO 1000): Reasonable play with some mistakes
  • Hard (ELO 1500): Good play, probably tough to beat
  • Expert (ELO 2100): Strong play
  • Ultra Difficult (ELO 2350): Maximum difficulty with advanced AI (highest available)

Player Settings

Choose who controls each color:

  • Blue (Computer): AI plays as blue
  • Red (Computer): AI plays as red
  • You can have both, one, or neither controlled by the AI

🔧 Other Features

Game Sequence (TRMPH)

The game uses a special notation called TRMPH to record moves:

  • Each move is recorded as a letter-number combination (e.g., "a1", "b2")
  • You can copy the current game sequence
  • You can apply a sequence of moves to jump to a specific position

🎲 Playing on Smaller Boards

While the default game uses a 13×13 board, you can also play on smaller boards for quicker games or different strategic challenges.

How to Use Smaller Boards

Use the Board Size setting in the main UI to pick the play area (top-left K×K).
